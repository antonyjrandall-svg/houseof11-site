The story

It starts on an island.

Four hundred years ago, an island of eleven parishes taught the world to turn cane into gold. Barbados made rum before anyone had a name for it — and made hosting a way of life before anyone called it hospitality. The island never shouted about either. It just kept doing both, better than anywhere on earth.

The House of 11 is that island, organised as a house — one standard, four doors.

Through the first door, the liquid: 11 Parishes, pure Barbados rum, aged in oak, nothing added. Through the second, the still: 11 Distillery, the source behind our bottles and yours. Through the third, the welcome: 11 Hospitality, the craft of rooms that earn their atmosphere, taught to the world. And through the fourth, the people: 11 Founders — a community founded by one hundred, connected through exceptional rum, memorable experiences and meaningful relationships.

The liquid carries the story. The still proves it. The welcome performs it. The founders carry it around the world — and once a year, the whole house comes home to the island.
