The origin · the founders

Four lifetimes. One goal.

One of us has spent a lifetime building some of the most exciting bars and restaurants in the world — and behind them, an exemplary record of creating some of the most extraordinary liquids ever poured.

One of us is rum's ambassador to the world — the voice that taught the world's great cities to take rum seriously, with a gift no one else alive has: creating rums that cannot be made by anyone else, for some of the most iconic musicians and creators on earth.

One of us has given a whole life to the making of rum itself — a lifetime of creating and producing spirit on the island where rum was born, work that collectors cross oceans to taste.

And one of us spent thirty years in culture — the last twenty at the intersection of sustainability and innovation, beside the artists who move the world and the scientists trying to save it.

We came together with one goal: to build the most innovative rum company ever made — a company that makes enough money to give it back to the roots and heritage of the very island that gave rum to the world.

As people drink the rum, the island rises. That is the whole company.

Barbados is the island of eleven parishes that taught the world to turn cane into gold. 11 is named for it — and built to repay it. We don't print our names on this page. Pour the rum, follow the story, and go find the characters.

The three turns of the loop

Serve

Rum, served like nowhere else

11 will stand at the forefront of how rum is served — in the world's great bars, through our bartender academy in Barbados, and through the Adventurers' Club, doing things on the progressive side of change that no one has done before.

Make

The distillery that runs on the sea

Barbados is already moving: a national wave-energy pilot has been signed for the island, and a university-born venture is turning distillery wastewater and sargassum seaweed into clean fuel. 11's distillery is being designed to plug into that future — powered and watered by the ocean, its by-products feeding the island's energy, aiming to be the most sustainable distillery in the world.

Give

Capital that goes home

The company is structured so that as 11 grows, investment, jobs and innovation flow back into Barbados — the academy, the distillery, the projects in our wake — until the island that made the rum becomes the most exciting place in the world.
